**Bily Apps — install capabilities, not scripts.**

Bily Apps is the app store for the internet: the installable capability layer of Bily. Bily connects and governs website data; Bily Apps lets teams activate reusable capabilities through that connection instead of rebuilding a separate script integration for every need.

## The Bily Apps lifecycle

<Steps>
  <Step title="Discover">
    Find a capability that matches the outcome the website needs.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Review permissions">
    Understand the data and actions the capability needs before activating it.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Configure">
    Provide the website-specific settings needed by that capability.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Install">
    Activate the capability through the site's existing Bily connection.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Observe">
    Review the status, results, and safe diagnostics that Bily makes available for the capability.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Update or revoke">
    Adopt a newer capability version or remove access without replacing the website's Bily integration.
  </Step>
</Steps>

## How Bily and Bily Apps differ

| Product | Role |
| --- | --- |
| Bily | The programmable customer data and activation layer that connects website, server, and AI workflows |
| Bily Apps | Installable capabilities activated through that governed Bily connection |

The Bily SDK, API, and MCP are developer surfaces for Bily. Bily Apps are the capabilities installed through the layer; the developer surfaces are not themselves Apps.

## Built for one connection

Bily Apps can cover analytics, advertising, customer data, and automation outcomes while keeping the website integration stable. A team can add or evolve a capability without asking every site owner to maintain another loader or release process.
